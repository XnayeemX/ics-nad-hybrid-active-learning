# Final Experiment Results

This directory contains the final outputs generated by the complete **research-mode** experiment for the comparison of **MLP**, **Random Forest**, and **XGBoost** under Random Sampling, Uncertainty Sampling, and the proposed **Hybrid Open-Set Active Learning** strategy.

The files in this folder are generated experiment artifacts and are included to make the reported results transparent and reproducible.

## Experiment Configuration

The final experiment used the following configuration:

- **Models:** MLP, Random Forest, XGBoost
- **Strategies:** Random Sampling, Uncertainty Sampling, Hybrid Open-Set Active Learning
- **Seeds:** 42, 2026, 31415
- **Initial labeled samples:** 50 per known class
- **Active Learning label budget:** 300
- **Query batch size:** 25
- **Hidden classes:** `land`, `ackpflood`, `teardrop`
- **Primary split:** `split_A_comparable`
- **External evaluation split:** `split_B_external`

The Hybrid query allocation was:

- Uncertainty: 8
- Novelty: 7
- Expansion: 7
- Random exploration: 3

The Hybrid acquisition score used:

- Uncertainty weight: 0.40
- Novelty weight: 0.40
- Density weight: 0.20
- Strict cosine-similarity threshold: 0.60

## Main Findings

### MLP — Fastest Hidden-Attack Discovery

MLP achieved the fastest full discovery of the hidden attack classes, requiring an average of approximately **25.3 queried labels**. It also achieved the lowest model-specific representation redundancy at approximately **0.171**.

However, its final multiclass performance was lower than the tree-based models, with a Macro-F1 of approximately **0.886**.

### Random Forest — Strongest Overall Balance

Random Forest achieved a strong balance between classification performance, hidden-class discovery efficiency, low redundancy, and external generalization.

Key results include:

- Macro-F1: approximately **0.994**
- Accuracy: approximately **0.993**
- Labels to full discovery: approximately **57.3**
- Unknown Query Yield: approximately **31.7%**
- Representation redundancy: approximately **0.178**
- External Macro-F1: approximately **0.889**

Random Forest achieved the highest external Macro-F1 among the three models.

### XGBoost — Strongest Final Classifier

XGBoost achieved the strongest final classification performance:

- Highest Accuracy: approximately **0.994**
- Highest Macro-F1: approximately **0.995**
- Hidden Macro Recall: **1.000**
- ACK Pair Macro-F1: approximately **0.996**
- Highest Unknown Query Yield: approximately **33.0%**

Its main limitation in the Hybrid Active Learning setting was its higher model-specific representation redundancy (**0.677**) and slower full hidden-class discovery (**129 labels on average**).

## Hybrid vs Random Sampling

The Hybrid strategy affected the three models differently.

| Model | Hybrid Labels to Discovery | Random Labels to Discovery | Labels Saved vs Random | Δ Macro-F1 | Δ Unknown Query Yield |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MLP | 25.333 | 90.667 | +65.333 | -0.0136 | +0.1867 |
| Random Forest | 57.333 | 90.667 | +33.333 | +0.0036 | +0.2322 |
| XGBoost | 129.000 | 90.667 | -38.333 | +0.0141 | +0.2456 |

The clearest overall Active Learning improvement was observed with **Random Forest**, where Hybrid reduced the number of labels required for full hidden-class discovery while also slightly improving Macro-F1 over Random Sampling.

MLP achieved the largest improvement in discovery speed, although its final Macro-F1 was slightly lower than its Random Sampling baseline.

XGBoost improved final classification performance and unknown-query yield under Hybrid, but required more labels for full discovery than Random Sampling.

## Hybrid Acquisition-Channel Effectiveness

The post-discovery **expansion** channel was the strongest source of hidden-class queries.

| Model | Channel | Queries | Hidden Queries | Hidden Yield |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MLP | Expansion | 231 | 201 | 0.870 |
| Random Forest | Expansion | 231 | 227 | 0.983 |
| XGBoost | Expansion | 231 | 231 | 1.000 |

This result supports the role of post-discovery expansion in converting initial discovery of a hidden class into additional labeled examples for learning that class.

## Target Checklist

All major predefined targets were achieved by MLP and Random Forest.

XGBoost achieved all listed targets except the model-specific representation redundancy target:

- Observed XGBoost redundancy: **0.677**
- Target: **< 0.60**

Because each model uses a different internal representation space, representation-redundancy values should not be treated as perfectly identical geometric quantities across models.

## Important Data Limitation

The hidden classes `land`, `ackpflood`, and `teardrop` each have only one independent source capture in the supplied dataset.

Therefore, this experiment evaluates their discovery and subsequent learning within the Active Learning setting, but does **not** claim source-file-disjoint generalization specifically for those hidden classes.
